How We Got Here

A timeline of trusted brands

OpenText Cybersecurity wasn't built from scratch — it was built by bringing together some of the most respected names in security under one roof. Each acquisition added a critical layer of protection:

2018
Erado joins Zix — compliance archiving & eDiscovery
2019
Webroot joins Carbonite — cloud-native endpoint protection & threat intelligence
2019
AppRiver joins Zix — MSP-first email security & Microsoft 365 services
2019
Carbonite (incl. Webroot) joins OpenText — SMB backup & endpoint footprint
2020
CloudAlly joins Zix — SaaS-to-SaaS backup for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Salesforce
2021
Zix (incl. AppRiver, CloudAlly, Erado) joins OpenText — unifying email security, compliance & backup
2024
Pillr joins OpenText — 24/7 Managed Detection & Response for MSPs

Zix Fully Absorbed

For decades, Zix was the trusted name in email encryption and secure communication for the Microsoft ecosystem. Zix joined OpenText in 2021 — same team, same expertise, now powering our email security line.

Webroot Business Side Only

AppRiver Fully Absorbed

Known for cloud email security and deep Microsoft 365 expertise, AppRiver built one of the industry's strongest MSP distribution channels — now part of our MSP-first model.
